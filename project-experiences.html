Projects

Research projects at the Metalcasting Research Lab, Korea Maritime and Ocean University, spanning residual stress, casting simulation, and aluminum alloy performance.

Residual Stress and Microstructure in Cast Aluminum Components

4D Residual Stress for Corrosion and Fatigue Design

Unveiling a 4D Residual-Stress Paradigm for Self-adaptive Corrosion and Fatigue Design in Automotive Aluminum Alloys

Mar. 2026 – Present · Project Participant
National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) · RS-2026-25493967

  • Coordinate laboratory experiments, analysis, and research progress for a project on residual stress in automotive aluminum alloys.
  • Use simulation-guided measurement locations, portable X-ray residual-stress analysis, and DOE to investigate stress metrics and microstructural factors.

Hybrid Die-Cast xEV Motor Housing

Development of an xEV Motor Housing Using Hybrid Die-Casting Technology

Sep. 2024 – Present · Project Participant
Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (MOTIE) · RS-2024-00433030

  • Take primary responsibility within the laboratory for coordinating experiments, analysis, and project progress.
  • Analyze MAGMA filling, solidification, residual-stress, and defect distributions under actual casting conditions.
  • Measure prototype residual stresses using portable X-ray analysis and assess simulation–experiment differences, including the effects of local cooling and squeeze application locations.

Lightweight Semi-Trailing Arm for xEVs

Development of a 30% Lightweight, High-Stiffness, and High-Durability Semi-Trailing Arm for Production xEVs

Jul. 2024 – Present · Project Participant
Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (MOTIE) · RS-2024-00470221

  • Measure residual stresses at locations susceptible to stress concentration and thermal deformation in counter-pressure-cast prototypes under different heat treatments.
  • Evaluate cooling-method effects on stress magnitude and direction, and relate measured stresses to local microstructure.

High-Vacuum Die-Cast Front Subframe

Development of High Vacuum Die Casting Process and Mold Technology with a Vacuum Degree of 70 mbar or Less for Rack Housing Integrated I-Type Front Subframe

Mar. 2024 – Present · Project Participant
Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (MOTIE) · RS-2022-KT219255

  • Coordinate laboratory experiments and analysis, selecting measurement locations from simulations and evaluating portable X-ray measurement reliability.
  • Compare simulated and measured residual stresses across stress metrics; von Mises stress showed the closest agreement in the full-scale subframe study.
  • Apply DOE to separate microstructural effects on residual stress, identifying Si sphericity as a statistically significant factor.

Simulation-Based Optimization of Semi-Solid Casting

Thin-Walled Components by Semi-Solid Casting

Development of Multi-Temperature-Control and Surface-Modified Die Technologies for Forming 2.5-mm-Class Thin-Walled Components Using Semi-Solid Casting

Mar. 2024 – Present · Project Participant
Korea Evaluation Institute of Industrial Technology (KEIT) · RS-2023-KT224924

  • Analyze semi-solid casting simulations to evaluate melt filling and flow behavior and propose conditions for reducing internal defects.
  • Apply DOE to evaluate combinations of cavity, neck, shape, and width variables for defect minimization.

Microstructure and Performance of Cast Aluminum Alloys and Coatings

Aluminum Alloys and Coatings for Brake Discs

Characterization of Cast Aluminum Alloys and Coating Materials for Automotive Brake Discs

Dec. 2024 – Jun. 2026 · Project Participant
Industry-sponsored research

  • Evaluated cast aluminum alloys and cermet coatings using microstructural analysis, hardness and surface-roughness measurements, and room- and high-temperature wear tests.
  • Assessed coating interfacial adhesion and corrosion behavior through electrochemical, salt-immersion, and galvanic corrosion tests.

Trace-Element Control in Recycled Aluminum

Development of Trace-Element Control Technology for Recycled Aluminum Alloys to Reduce Carbon Emissions

Aug. 2025 – Feb. 2026 · Project Participant
Industry-sponsored research

  • Evaluated composition-dependent castability, mechanical properties, and corrosion behavior through fluidity, tensile, impact, and electrochemical tests.
  • Used optical microscopy and SEM–EDS to investigate microstructural evolution and intermetallic phases across alloy compositions and heat treatments.

Aluminum Machining Scrap Recycling

Development of an Environmentally Friendly Aluminum Machining Scrap Recycling Process to Improve Yield by 10%

Mar. 2024 – Mar. 2025 · Project Participant
Korea Technology and Information Promotion Agency for SMEs (TIPA) · RS-2023-0022652

  • Assessed alloy-composition effects on castability, mechanical properties, and corrosion behavior using fluidity, tensile, impact, and electrochemical tests.
  • Characterized microstructure and intermetallic phases using optical microscopy and SEM–EDS to support recycling-process development.
