Projects

Residual Stress in Cast Aluminum Components

Residual Stress Analysis and Mitigation in High-Vacuum Die Casting

Investigated residual stress in high-vacuum die-cast aluminum front subframes to identify strategies for stress mitigation.

Research Contributions

  • Mapped residual stress distributions through casting simulations and location-specific X-ray measurements.
  • Compared simulated and measured stresses and assessed measurement reliability.
  • Investigated microstructural factors associated with residual stress and local variations in mechanical properties.

Residual Stress and Distortion Analysis in Hybrid Die Casting

Investigated residual stress and distortion in hybrid die-cast xEV motor housings through simulation and experimental measurements.

Research Contributions

  • Analyzed filling, solidification, residual stress, and defect distributions using MAGMA software under actual casting process conditions.
  • Measured residual stress at simulation-selected locations and assessed discrepancies between predictions and measurements.
  • Investigated factors governing casting distortion and evaluated external cooling and squeeze conditions for residual stress mitigation.

Effects of Heat Treatment and Microstructure on Residual Stress in Counter-Pressure Casting

Examined the relationships between heat treatment, microstructure, and residual stress in counter-pressure-cast semi-trailing arms.

Research Contributions

  • Characterized residual stress across alloy and heat-treatment conditions at locations susceptible to stress concentration and thermal distortion.
  • Examined how cooling conditions affected residual stress magnitude and direction.
  • Related local microstructural characteristics to measured residual stress and investigated stress development mechanisms.

Simulation-Based Optimization of Semi-Solid Casting

Process and Geometry Optimization for Defect Control in Semi-Solid Casting

Investigated process and geometry conditions for defect control in semi-solid-cast shock absorber housings and motor support housings.

Research Contributions

  • Analyzed filling and solidification behavior to investigate defect formation across process conditions and component geometries.
  • Applied design of experiments (DOE) to evaluate cavity, neck, and other geometry-related variables affecting defect formation.
  • Identified process and geometry conditions to improve filling behavior and reduce predicted internal defects.
Project details
  1. Development of Multi-Temperature-Control and Surface-Modified Die Technologies for Forming 2.5-mm-Class Thin-Walled Components Using Semi-Solid Casting — Korea Evaluation Institute of Industrial Technology, RS-2023-KT224924 (Mar. 2024 – Present)

Alloy Development and Surface Performance

Effects of Trace Elements on Recycled Aluminum Alloy Performance

Evaluated recycled aluminum alloys to support trace-element control technology development.

Research Contributions

  • Evaluated composition-dependent castability, mechanical properties, and corrosion behavior through fluidity, tensile, impact, and electrochemical tests.
  • Characterized microstructure and intermetallic phases using optical microscopy and SEM/EDS across alloy compositions and heat treatments.

Wear and Corrosion Performance of Aluminum Alloys and Cermet Coatings

Characterized cast aluminum alloys and cermet coatings for automotive brake discs, focusing on wear, adhesion, and corrosion performance.

Research Contributions

  • Evaluated cast aluminum alloys and cermet coatings using microstructural analysis, hardness and surface-roughness measurements, and room- and high-temperature wear tests.
  • Assessed coating interfacial adhesion and corrosion behavior through electrochemical, salt-immersion, and galvanic corrosion tests.
Project details
  1. Characterization of Cast Aluminum Alloys and Coating Materials for Automotive Brake Discs — Industry-Sponsored Research Project (Dec. 2024 – Jun. 2026)
  2. Development of Trace-Element Control Technology for Recycled Aluminum Alloys to Reduce Carbon Emissions — Industry-Sponsored Research Project (Aug. 2025 – Feb. 2026)
  3. Development of an Environmentally Friendly Aluminum Machining Scrap Recycling Process to Improve Yield by 10% — Korea Technology and Information Promotion Agency for SMEs, RS-2023-0022652 (Mar. 2024 – Mar. 2025)
